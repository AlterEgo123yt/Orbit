# Orbit  
 
 **The Central Hub for Software, Design & Creative Projects** 
  
   Welcome to **Orbit**—a personal umbrella organization that I designed to anchor all of my builds, experiments, and creative endeavors under one cohesive identity. Rather than letting my individual ideas exist in isolation, Orbit serves as the central axis that brings them all together, unifying software engineering, visual design, and interactive concepts through a shared vision and consistent aesthetic. Every app hosted here represents a different project, but they are all still tied together by the same standards of craft and design.

  **Core Mission:** Constructing intuitive tools, clean visual systems, and thoughtful digital experiences.
